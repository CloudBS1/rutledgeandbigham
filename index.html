Planning a Funeral in Statesville, North Carolina

When someone dies, there is no need to settle every detail at once. Start with the immediate call, then make the decisions that shape a meaningful gathering. This guide helps families organize those steps and connect with Rutledge & Bigham Mortuary in Statesville.

Begin with the immediate steps

If a death has just occurred, contact the appropriate medical professional or emergency service as the situation requires, then notify the funeral home. Share the name of the person who died, where they are, and the best number for a family contact. The funeral director can explain the next arrangements and what information will be needed. Keep important documents and family contact details together; you can gather the remaining preferences in stages.

Decide what kind of gathering feels right

A funeral may include a visitation, a ceremony, and burial or cremation. A memorial or celebration of life may be arranged on a different timeline. Consider the person’s wishes, faith and cultural traditions, the number of guests, and whether relatives need time to travel to Statesville. Ask which parts can take place at the mortuary, a place of worship, or another suitable location.

Make the service personal

Choose a few details that tell the person’s story: music, photographs, a reading, an obituary, or a speaker who knew them well. Rutledge & Bigham describes chapel, visitation, coffee lounge, and reception facilities; ask the team which spaces fit your plans and anticipated attendance.

Review costs before confirming

Ask for an itemized explanation of the services and merchandise under consideration, including any third-party costs. Compare what is essential for your family with what is optional. Write down questions about timing, transportation, viewing, and the place of final disposition before the arrangement meeting.

Frequently asked questions

What should I do first after a death in Statesville?

Attend to any immediate medical or emergency needs, then call the funeral home. The funeral director can guide you through the next steps and the information to prepare.

Can a funeral include a personalized celebration?

Yes. Families can discuss readings, music, photographs, and a gathering format that reflects the person they are remembering.
